# Privacy Policy

- The extension runs entirely in your browser.
- Email content is analyzed **locally**. No content leaves your device unless you enable external checks.
- When external checks are enabled:
  - **URLs only** are sent to the providers you enable.
  - **The sender email only** is sent to Have I Been Pwned (if enabled).
- API keys are stored in `chrome.storage.local`. They are never synced to your Google account.
- History and whitelist are stored locally and can be cleared from Options → Advanced.
- We do not operate any server. We do not collect telemetry.
